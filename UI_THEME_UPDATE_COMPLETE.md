## 🎨 Design System

### Colors
- **Primary**: Black (`#000000`)
- **Background**: White (`#FFFFFF`)
- **Secondary**: Gray shades (`#F9FAFB`, `#F3F4F6`, `#E5E7EB`)
- **Accents**: Green (positive), Red (negative), Yellow (warning)

### Typography
- **Font**: Inter (Google Fonts)
- **Headings**: Bold, large (text-3xl to text-7xl)
- **Body**: Regular weight, readable sizes

### Components
- **Buttons**: Black background, white text, rounded-lg
- **Inputs**: White background, gray border, black focus ring
- **Cards**: White background, gray border, subtle shadows
- **Sidebar**: White background, gray borders, gray active states

### Animations
- **Fade In Up**: Content entrance
- **Float**: Floating elements
- **Gradient**: Animated backgrounds
- **Hover Lift**: Cards lift on hover
- **Hover Scale**: Icons scale on hover
- **Ripple**: Button click effect
