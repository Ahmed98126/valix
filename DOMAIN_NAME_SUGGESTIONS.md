## 🔍 **How to Check Availability**

### **Step 1: Check Domain Availability**
Go to:
- **Namecheap.com** (recommended)
- **Google Domains**
- **GoDaddy.com**

Search for your preferred name.

### **Step 2: Check Alternative Extensions**
If `.com` is taken, try:
- `.io` (tech-friendly, modern)
- `.app` (SaaS-friendly)
- `.co` (short, modern)
- `.ai` (if AI-focused)

**Example:**
- InvoiceFlow.com (if taken)
- InvoiceFlow.io ✅
- InvoiceFlow.app ✅

### **Step 3: Register**
- **Recommended**: Namecheap (~$10-15/year)
- **Alternative**: Google Domains (~$12/year)
- **Azure**: Azure Domain Services (~$15/year)
